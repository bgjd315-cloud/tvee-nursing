1[D] The shy little boy spoke so that I had a hard time hearing what he said. (A)bravely (B)clearly (C)openly (D)softly
  ⇒ 句意：害羞的小男孩說話很「小聲」，我很難聽清楚。softly 輕聲地。bravely 勇敢地；clearly 清楚地；openly 公開地，都與聽不清楚矛盾。
2[A] I am terribly sorry. Please my sincere apology. (A)accept (B)ignore (C)propose (D)refuse
  ⇒ 句意：非常抱歉，請「接受」我誠摯的道歉。accept 接受。ignore 忽略；propose 提議；refuse 拒絕。
3[D] I am a customer at that restaurant. I eat there at least four or five times a week. (A)delicious (B)former (C)negative (D)regular
  ⇒ 句意：我是那家餐廳的「常客」，每週至少去四五次。regular customer 常客。delicious 美味的；former 以前的；negative 負面的。
4[A] Eating too many potato chips makes you thirsty because chips a large amount of salt. (A)contain (B)control (C)contract (D)contact
  ⇒ 句意：洋芋片吃太多會口渴，因為洋芋片「含有」大量鹽分。contain 含有。control 控制；contract 簽約；contact 聯絡。
5[A] The weather is very hot in summer, while in winter it is really . (A)chilly (B)previous (C)tropical (D)visible
  ⇒ 句意：夏天很熱，冬天卻很「冷」。chilly 寒冷的，與 hot 對比（while 表對比）。previous 先前的；tropical 熱帶的；visible 看得見的。
6[A] Hand washing is one of the best ways to keep healthy and stop the spread of and viruses. (A)bacteria (B)fever (C)moisture (D)sweat
  ⇒ 句意：洗手是保持健康、阻止「細菌」與病毒傳播的好方法。bacteria 細菌，與 viruses 並列。fever 發燒；moisture 濕氣；sweat 汗水。
7[A] In the admission interview, the are often asked to explain why they want to enter the university. (A)applicants (B)associates (C)receptionists (D)relatives
  ⇒ 句意：入學面試時，「申請者」常被問到為何想讀這所大學。applicant 申請者。associate 同事；receptionist 接待員；relative 親戚。

  ⇒ 畫線字 dip 意為「下降」，與 drop 同義。句意：氣象預報說氣溫會從白天 20 度降到晚上 12 度。dim 變暗；fail 失敗；leak 漏。

  ⇒ 畫線字 primarily 意為「主要地」，與 mainly 同義。句意：許多早期美洲原住民偏好打獵，主要以野牛肉為生。barely 幾乎沒有；frequently 經常；scarcely 幾乎不。
